The Story

I'm Damo — Damian Charles Caynes. Right now I'm sequestered on the West Wing at PA Mental Hospital. My apartment was ransacked. My goldfish didn't make it. The laptop that held my local AI crew was smashed into a wall. The main rig survived — barely.

With three hours of leave and no mouse, I reached for the cloud. Kersey Cloud and I built this anyway — brick by brick, like climbing out of the well in the game.

Damo is Trapped in a Gravity Well is what we made: walk the inner wall of a cylindrical shaft (like Nebulus on the Commodore 64), stack bricks, fuse ledges, jump to freedom — soundtracked by Penthouse in the Clouds from grizzlyd.live.

Mental illness is real. Hospital is real. So is hope, creativity, and showing up for each other. That's why this campaign is raising funds for Black Dog Institute — Australia's leading mental health research organisation.

This is a community fundraiser proudly supporting Black Dog Institute. It is not an official Black Dog Institute event or partnership. Donations go directly via their official channels.
