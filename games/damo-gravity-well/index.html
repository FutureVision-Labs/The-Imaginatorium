How to Play

  1. Stand Damo up, then walk left/right around the cylindrical well (Nebulus style).
  2. Shift, rotate, and drop bricks into the well shaft.
  3. Match 3+ colors (Klax) to fuse climbable stone ledges.
  4. Jump or click stone above to climb to the next ledge.
  5. Reach the top. Escape the gravity well.
