How to Play

  1. Shift & rotate falling bricks (Tetris).
  2. Drop them into the well.
  3. Match 3+ same-color bricks in a row or column (Klax) — they fuse into climbable stone.
  4. Stand Damo up, then click fused stone above him to climb.
  5. Reach the top. Escape the well.
